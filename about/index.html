Who We Are

Your Trusted Insurance Partner in Canada

Meet My Advisor is an independent insurance brokerage founded on the principle that every Canadian deserves access to clear, expert, and unbiased financial guidance. We work with Canada's leading insurance providers to bring you the most competitive rates and comprehensive coverage options available in the market.

Our team of licensed advisors brings decades of combined experience across all major insurance categories — life, health, travel, disability, critical illness, funeral planning, and estate management. We take a holistic approach to your financial wellbeing, ensuring that your coverage grows with you as your life changes.

We are committed to full transparency. There are no hidden fees, no pressure tactics, and no one-size-fits-all policies. We listen first, then recommend coverage that genuinely fits your life.
