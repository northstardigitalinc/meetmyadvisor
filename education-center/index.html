Insurance & Financial Education Resources

Knowledge is the foundation of good financial decisions. Explore our resources to better understand your options and rights as a Canadian insurance consumer.

Understanding Life Insurance

Learn the difference between term and permanent life insurance, how death benefits work, and how to determine the right coverage amount for your family.

RESP – Education Savings Explained

The Registered Education Savings Plan (RESP) lets you save for your child's post-secondary education. The government offers up to $9200 in free grants — learn how to maximize them.

How Critical Illness Insurance Works

Understand what qualifies as a critical illness, how the lump-sum payment is triggered, and why this coverage is essential for working Canadians.

Travel Insurance: What's Covered?

Many Canadians assume their credit card or provincial health plan covers them abroad. Learn what gaps exist and what a proper travel insurance policy actually protects.

Disability Insurance Basics

Estate Planning Fundamentals

What happens to your assets when you pass away? Learn how life insurance can help your estate avoid probate fees, cover final tax liabilities, and preserve your legacy.
